How it works

Understanding the forecast

Swell

Wave energy arriving from offshore. Height tells you how much energy there is, period tells you how long the waves are spaced apart, and direction tells you where the swell is coming from.

Wind

Wind speed and direction at Barleycove. Northerly and north-easterly winds are generally the most favourable because they are offshore for the beach.

Tide

The predicted water level and whether the tide is rising or falling. Barleycove can work on different tides, but a low-to-rising tide is generally given a small advantage.

Surf score

The score combines swell direction, exposure, height, period, wind direction, wind speed and tide. It is a guide for Barleycove, not a guarantee of what the waves will look like.

Forecast confidence

Confidence is based on how much of the forecast data is available. High means the main swell and wind inputs are present, while lower confidence means some inputs are missing.

Map

The map shows the Barleycove surf point and the current selected swell direction. The closer the swell is to the beach's useful exposure, the better the exposure part of the score.
